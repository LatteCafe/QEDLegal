QED Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Last updated: October 24, 2026

QED (the "Bot") is a Discord application that provides mathematics tools and a daily problem. It is operated by Ethan Zhang ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Bot handles, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what choices you have. It applies whenever you use the Bot's commands or buttons, in a Discord server or wherever else Discord allows the Bot to be used, and whenever a server you belong to uses it.

Summary

  • We store very little. For the Problem of the Day we keep a Discord user ID together with counts and timestamps. We do not store your name, email address, the text of your answers, or your messages.
  • Calculator and LaTeX inputs are not saved. They are used to produce a reply and then discarded. The reply, including an echo of your input, is posted in the channel, so other people there can see it.
  • No selling, no advertising, no tracking, no AI training. The Bot sends nothing to any online service other than Discord.
  • We do not delete anything automatically. Removing the Bot from a server does not erase that server's records. You can ask us to delete your records (or your server's) at any time. See Section 8.

1. Information we handle

1.1 Information stored in our database (Problem of the Day)

Data What it contains Why we keep it
Server settings Server (guild) ID, the channel chosen for posts, posting time, timezone, difficulty level, "no repeats within N days" setting, and whether posting is enabled. Set by server administrators. To post the daily problem at the right time and place.
Post records Server ID, channel ID, Discord message ID, which problem was posted, and the date. To avoid duplicate posts, to attach the buttons to the right problem, and to avoid repeating problems.
Participation records Your Discord user ID, linked to a post, with: the number of hints you used, the number of answer attempts, the time of your last attempt, the time you solved it, and the time you viewed the solution. To enforce attempt limits and cooldowns, to record solves, and to calculate streaks and leaderboards.

A Discord user ID is a number that identifies an account. Because it can identify you, we treat it as personal information.

Not stored: the text of the answers you submit (they are checked and then discarded; only counts and timestamps are kept), your username, display name or avatar, your email address, your IP address, and the content of any message.

1.2 Information processed but not stored (calculator, LaTeX and matrix commands)

The expressions, equations, matrices and LaTeX you type into commands are used to compute and draw a result. They are processed in memory by the Bot and by short-lived helper processes that run with strict time and memory limits and without access to the Bot's credentials, and are then discarded. They are not written to our database.

Replies to these commands are posted in the channel. They include an echo of what you typed and are visible to everyone who can see that channel, and Discord keeps them under its own policies. Please do not enter personal or confidential information into commands.

Replies from the Problem of the Day buttons (hints, answer checks, solutions) and from /potd stats, /potd leaderboard and /potd status are sent as private "ephemeral" messages that only the person who asked can see. The daily problem post itself is public but contains no information about users.

What other members can see: any member of a server can ask the Bot to show another member's Problem of the Day record (problems solved, streaks, solves without hints) in a reply only the asker sees. /potd leaderboard lists the server's top solvers by Discord mention.

1.3 Information we receive from Discord

When you use the Bot, Discord sends it your user ID and display name, the server, channel and message IDs, your permissions in that channel, and the options you entered. We use IDs to operate the features and may show your display name back to you in a reply (for example in /potd stats). We do not store display names. While running, the Bot keeps a temporary in-memory copy of some server and channel information supplied by Discord.

The Bot does not request Discord's privileged "Message Content" permission and does not read or store the content of messages. It accepts no file uploads.

1.4 Technical logs

The computer that runs the Bot keeps technical logs: timestamps, command names, server, channel and message IDs, and error details. Logs are not intended to record who ran a command. When something fails, a log line can include a short fragment derived from input, for example up to 200 characters of a result's LaTeX if drawing it fails, or the error output of a calculation that crashed. We use logs only to fix bugs and keep the Bot secure, and keep them for 30 days.

1.5 What we do not collect

Real names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, location, contacts, voice or video, uploaded files, advertising identifiers, or cookies. (The Bot has no website of its own and sets no cookies.)

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • provide the Bot's features and keep Problem of the Day records, streaks and leaderboards;
  • enforce attempt limits, cooldowns and size limits, and prevent abuse;
  • keep the Bot running and secure, and find and fix bugs;
  • respond to legal requests and comply with the law; and
  • announce important changes to the Bot or these documents.

We do not sell or rent personal information, show advertising, build advertising profiles, make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, or use your inputs or records to train artificial-intelligence models. (As of the effective date the Bot does not use any AI model; its calculations are performed by conventional mathematical software running on our own server.)

3. Legal bases (where GDPR or UK GDPR applies)

  • Performing the service you ask for: carrying out your commands and recording your participation in the Problem of the Day.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the Bot secure, preventing abuse, fixing bugs, and keeping the limited records needed for streaks and leaderboards. We have weighed these against your rights, and the data involved is minimal.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.
  • Consent: by pressing the Problem of the Day buttons or submitting an answer you ask us to record your participation. You can withdraw this by stopping use and asking us to delete your records.

4. Who we share information with

  • Discord Inc. The Bot runs on Discord, which processes everything you send through it under Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting provider (Oracle Cloud, Canada East). It hosts the computer that runs the Bot and its database, and processes data on our behalf.
  • Other members of a server, as described in Section 1.2.
  • Authorities or others, where required by law, or where necessary to protect people's rights, safety or property.
  • A successor, if the Bot is transferred to someone else, in which case the data goes with it and this policy continues to apply until it is updated.

We do not share information with advertisers, data brokers or analytics providers, and the Bot does not send what you type to any other online service.

5. International transfers

Our data is stored with Oracle Cloud, Canada East. Discord operates in the United States and other countries. Your information may therefore be processed in a country other than your own. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on those provided by Discord and our hosting provider (for example standard contractual clauses).

6. How long we keep information

Information Retention
Server settings, post records and participation records Until deleted. We do not currently delete these automatically, including when the Bot is removed from a server. We delete them when you or the server's administrators ask (Section 8), within 30 days of a verified request.
Technical logs 30 days
Backups of the database 30 days. Deleted records disappear from backups when those backups expire.
Calculator, LaTeX and matrix inputs Not stored. Held in memory only while a result is produced. (A fragment can appear in a log if something fails; see 1.4.)

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold:

  • the Bot's access token and other secrets are kept in configuration outside the source code;
  • calculations run in separate helper processes with time and memory limits and without access to those secrets;
  • the Bot asks Discord only for the permissions it needs, and holds only the minimal records described above;
  • access to the computer and database is restricted to the operator.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will tell you as the law requires.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or Canadian privacy law) you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy of it, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, contact us at qed@ezhang.ca or through https://discord.gg/n6cTx5fD6 and include your Discord user ID (and the server ID for a request about a server's records). We may ask you to prove you control the account, for example by sending a message from it, and for a server-wide request, that you hold the Manage Server permission there. We will respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires.

Choices you can make yourself:

  • Stop using the Bot at any time. If you never press the Problem of the Day buttons, no participation record is created for you.
  • Server administrators can turn off the daily post (/potd setup enabled:False) or remove the Bot from the server.
  • There is currently no setting to hide your record from /potd stats and /potd leaderboard. If you would like to be removed, contact us and we will delete your participation records.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law, and we have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information.

9. Children

Discord requires its users to be at least 13 years old, or older where the law of their country sets a higher age. The Bot is not directed at children below that age and we do not knowingly collect their information. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. Parents or guardians who believe this has happened can contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, announce them at https://discord.gg/n6cTx5fD6. If you keep using the Bot after a change takes effect, you accept the updated policy.
